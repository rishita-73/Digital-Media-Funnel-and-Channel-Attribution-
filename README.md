# Digital Media Funnel & Channel Attribution

An end-to-end analytics project modeling how card-linked offers platform would evaluate its digital media channels from impression through click, offer view, and redemption to answer a media-budget question with SQL, Python, and a Power BI dashboard.

## Business problem
Card-Linked offers platform distributes offers across multiple digital channels push
notifications, email, in-app banners, SMS, social ads, display ads but
spend isn't necessarily going where it converts best. High-reach paid
channels (Display, Social) aren't automatically the most efficient; owned
channels (Push, In-App) often convert far better per dollar. Without a
funnel + CAC/ROI view, budget gets allocated by reach or habit, not
efficiency.


 ## 1. What each step does

**Step 1 — data generation.** Builds three linked tables: 
Generates the synthetic dataset used for the project:

- `customers` — customer information, 
- `channels` — six digital marketing channels and their associated costs  (Push Notification, In-App Banner, Email, SMS,
Social Media Ad, Display Ad — each with a real cost-per-impression and
funnel-stage base rates).
- `funnel_exposures` — 80,000 impression-level records tracking progression through click, offer view, and redemption

**Step 2 — SQLite load.** Loads the generated datasets into a local **SQLite database** (`media_funnel.db`) for SQL-based analysis.

**Step 3 — SQL analysis** (`sql/analysis_queries.sql`): 
Runs SQL queries to analyze:

- Overall funnel conversion
- Funnel performance by channel
- CPC, cost-per-view, and CAC
- Funnel drop-offs
- Redemption by category and channel
- Monthly redemption trends
- Best-performing channel by category

**Step 4 — channel attribution & reallocation simulation.** Ranks channels based on **cost per redemption (CAC)** and performs a **what-if budget reallocation analysis** to evaluate the potential impact of shifting spend toward higher-performing channels.

**Step 5 — charts.** 
Generates charts covering:

- Digital funnel performance
- CAC by channel
- Redemption rate by channel
- Funnel retention by channel
